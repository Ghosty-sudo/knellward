# KNELLWARD — Steam Store Draft

## Short description
Descend beneath a dead bell-tower city in a compact turn-based pixel RPG. Build your Knellwarden with relics, manage Focus, read enemy telegraphs, and silence the Bell Below before it tolls your name.

## About This Game
The city stopped answering at dawn. The bell did not.

KNELLWARD is a small, replayable dark-fantasy RPG where every step is a turn. Descend through five remixed dungeon depths, strike enemies by moving into them, spend Focus on decisive abilities, and shape each run with relics that change how you survive.

### Every step matters
Movement, attacks, abilities, healing, and enemy actions share the same turn economy. Position well, read telegraphed danger, and decide when to spend your limited Focus.

### Build a different Warden
Level up during the descent and choose permanent run relics between depths. Lean into raw damage, defense, Focus recovery, critical strikes, sustain, or safer positioning.

### Silence the Bell Below
A complete campaign leads through five increasingly dangerous depths to a multi-phase final encounter. Victory unlocks Endless Descent for score chasing and deeper scaling.

### Designed to finish, designed to replay
A successful campaign targets a compact single sitting rather than a sprawling unfinished epic. Runs change through remixed layouts, enemy mixes, elite spawns, relic choices, and difficulty settings.

## Feature bullets
- Turn-based grid combat where movement is part of the battle
- Five-depth campaign with final boss and ending
- XP, levels, abilities, consumables, and run-defining relics
- Multiple enemy archetypes and elite variants
- Unlockable Endless Descent
- Three difficulty settings
- Keyboard, mouse-menu, and basic gamepad support
- Save/resume, persistent settings, and local records
- Synthesized original audio; no network connection required
- Reduced-motion, screen-shake, and high-contrast options
